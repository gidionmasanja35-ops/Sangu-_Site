FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions about
Sangu African Safari

Have questions about planning your Tanzania safari or mountain trek? Here are answers to the most common queries from our travelers.

Sangu African Safari is a 100% locally owned Tanzanian safari specialist based in Arusha. We provide tailor-made itineraries, private customized 4x4 Land Cruisers with pop-up roofs, expert local guides with decades of tracking experience, and handpicked luxury tented camps and lodges.

Budget and mid-range options offer exceptional value without compromising on the wildlife viewing experience. You get the exact same private 4x4 vehicle, expert national park guides, and safari circuits as luxury tours, while staying in clean, comfortable, and authentic safari camps or lodges.

The dry season from June to October is the best time for general wildlife viewing and Kilimanjaro climbing. December to March is ideal for the Serengeti Wildebeest Calving Season in Ndutu, while July to October is prime time to witness the dramatic Mara River crossings during the Great Migration.

Our northern circuit packages cover Serengeti National Park, Ngorongoro Crater, Tarangire National Park, Lake Manyara, and Arusha National Park. We also offer custom routes to Nyerere (Selous) and Ruaha in Southern Tanzania.

Yes, absolutely! We seamlessly arrange fly-in bush-to-beach extensions where you can fly straight from the Serengeti or Arusha airstrip to Zanzibar for beach relaxation, diving, and Stone Town tours.

Prices vary depending on the season, duration, travel style (Budget, Mid-Range, or Luxury), and group size. Budget safaris range from $200–$350 per day per person, mid-range safaris from $380–$600, while luxury fly-in safaris range from $700–$1,500+ per day. Contact us for an exact personalized quote.

Tanzania is one of Africa’s safest and most peaceful destinations. Your safety is our highest priority—our professional guides manage all park logistics, private transfers, and lodge stays from the moment you arrive at Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO).

We recommend lightweight neutral-colored clothing (khaki, beige, green), a warm jacket for early morning game drives and mountain nights, sturdy walking shoes, sunglasses, sunblock, insect repellent, binoculars, and a quality camera. We send a comprehensive packing list upon booking!

Simply click any "Book Now" or "Start Your Trip" button on our website, fill out your travel dates and group details, or chat with us directly via WhatsApp. Our safari team will send you a tailored itinerary and quote within 24 hours!

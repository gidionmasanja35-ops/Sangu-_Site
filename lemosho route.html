Mount Kilimanjaro

Lemosho Route

The Lemosho Route offers a perfect balance of breathtaking landscapes, rich biodiversity, and a high summit success rate. It's considered one of the most beautiful and less crowded routes to the Roof of Africa.

7 Days Trek Duration
5,895 m Summit Altitude
Moderate Difficulty
Book This Route
Route Highlights

Unforgettable Landscapes at Every Step

Lush Rainforest

Begin your journey through misty forests, home to unique wildlife and flora.

Shira Plateau

Trek across the expansive Shira Plateau with panoramic views.

Barranco Wall

A thrilling section with stunning views and a true adventure experience.

Uhuru Peak

Reach the summit of 5,895m and stand on the highest point in Africa.

Quick Info

  • Duration 7 Days / 6 Nights
  • Distance ~ 70 km (total)
  • Summit Altitude 5,895 m
  • Difficulty Moderate
  • Best Season Jan – Mar, Jun – Oct
Day by Day

The Lemosho Itinerary

DAY01

Londorossi Gate → Mti Mkubwa Camp

10 km 1,800 m → 2,650 m

Begin your trek through the lush rainforest to Mti Mkubwa Camp.

DAY02

Mti Mkubwa → Shira 1 Camp

8 km 2,650 m → 3,500 m

Leave the forest and enter the moorland, with stunning views of Kibo and Shira Plateau.

DAY03

Shira Plateau → Barranco Camp

10 km 3,500 m → 3,950 m

Short hike and rest day to help your body acclimatise properly.

DAY04

Barranco → Karanga Camp

5 km 3,950 m → 4,035 m

Climb the Barranco Wall and continue to Karanga Camp.

What's Included

  • Professional mountain guides
  • Camping equipment & meals
  • Park fees & permits
  • Transport (to & from the mountain)
  • Emergency support & first aid

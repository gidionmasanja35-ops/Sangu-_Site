Follow the Great Wildebeest Migration

Join one of the greatest wildlife spectacles on earth. Over eight days you will track the wildebeest herds across the Serengeti plains, witness the dramatic river crossings, and descend into the Ngorongoro Crater to see the Big Five up close.

Stay in premium camps and lodges, guided every step of the way by local experts who know the migration routes and the stories behind them.
